Hello! I'm Hali and I love reading webcomics. My first ever webcomic "Noisy Neighbors" was based on a suggestion from a friend. It is supposed to be a humorous "gag-of-the-day" comic that you might enjoy on the bus ride home or before your lock-in session. I liked using a "flat" aesthetic in my comic to emphasize its humor. Thanks for reading!
